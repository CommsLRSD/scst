General Information for Education Job Applicants

  • Applicants are encouraged to apply to any teaching or education-related posting that interests them.
  • Please quote the bulletin number on each application.
  • Submit a new and complete application/resume for every job posting or bulletin of interest.
  • Follow the Application Procedure listed on each job posting.
  • Do not send applications to the general Louis Riel School Division email address or to individual Human Resources staff members.
  • An offer of employment with LRSD is subject to the results of a Criminal Record Check and a clear report from the Child Abuse Registry.
  • Only those under consideration will be contacted for an interview.
  • Applicants for teaching positions must hold a permanent or provisional Teaching Certificate or a Limited Teaching Permit issued by Manitoba Education.
